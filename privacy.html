Glidra Privacy Policy

Glidra is a personal paper-plane flight journal. This policy explains how the app handles information on your device.

What Glidra stores locally

Glidra is designed to keep your hobby notes on your iPhone. Depending on how you use the app, it may store:

  • Flight titles, model names, results, distances, times, and free-form observation notes
  • Model workshop profiles such as paper size and fold notes
  • Optional photos you capture or select as reference images
  • Optional voice notes and speech-to-text drafts you create
  • Local gift-balance and purchase history metadata for consumable in-app gifts

These items remain in on-device storage unless you export, share, clear local data, or remove the app.

Permissions

  • Camera — requested only when you choose to take a reference photo
  • Photo Library — requested only when you choose an existing photo or save an image
  • Microphone — requested only when you record a voice note or start speech-to-text
  • Speech Recognition — requested only when you convert speech into editable text

Glidra does not use App Tracking Transparency and does not consume advertising identifiers for profiling.

Purchases

Optional gift packs are processed by Apple through StoreKit. Apple handles payment credentials. Glidra stores only the local purchase markers needed to show gift balance and history on this device. There is no in-app restore flow for consumable gifts.

Accounts and networking

Quick Login creates a local session on this device. Glidra does not require a cloud account to record flights. Opening Privacy Policy or Terms of Use inside the app loads the published pages over HTTPS.

Data control

  • Log Out returns you to the guide screen and ends the local session
  • Clear Local Data removes flights, models, media files, purchases, and settings stored by Glidra on this device

Children

Glidra is intended as a general hobby utility. Do not submit personal information of children where local laws require parental consent you cannot provide.

Contact

For privacy questions about Glidra, use the support channel listed on the App Store product page.
